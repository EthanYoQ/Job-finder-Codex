# Require explicit mainland location evidence for China-scoped jobs

Job Finder treats a job as China-scoped only when archived official evidence explicitly names mainland China, China nationwide, or a mainland city. Regional descriptors such as APAC may be retained as supporting text when mainland evidence is also present, but they cannot substitute for it in normalized output, completeness proof, or Stable Company qualification; this deliberately prefers fail-closed evidence over broader but ambiguous regional coverage.
